Speaking Study

Thank you for taking part. In this study you will read 21 short English sentences aloud, one at a time, and your voice will be recorded.

It takes about 10 minutes. Please sit somewhere quiet and use the same device from start to finish.

If you close the page by accident, open the same link again in the same browser and you will continue where you left off.

Microphone check

Your browser will ask for permission to use the microphone. Please choose Allow.

Say a few words, for example: “Hello, this is a test.”

Listening…

  • Sit about 20–30 cm (8–12 inches) from the microphone.
  • Keep the same distance for every sentence.

How it works

  1. Read the sentence to yourself first.
  2. Press Record and read the sentence aloud once, naturally, as if you were saying it to a friend.
  3. Press Stop & save. Your recording is saved and the next sentence appears.

If you stumble, press Restart and read the sentence again from the beginning.

Tip: you can also press Space to start and to stop.
